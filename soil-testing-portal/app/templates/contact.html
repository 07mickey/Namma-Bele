❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does sample collection work?

After booking an appointment, an authorized field technician visits your farm plot on the selected date and time slot. They take 5–8 zigzag soil cores at a standard 15 cm root depth, composite the sample, seal it with a barcode code (e.g., SMP-2026-00042), and deliver it to the district laboratory.

How do I track my soil sample?

You can track the complete 13-stage journey in real-time from your Farmer Portal under Live Sample Tracking or by visiting the tracking page with your sample code.

How do I receive my physical Soil Health Card?

Once authorized by an agricultural scientist, the official Soil Health Card is printed and dispatched via India Post Speed Post directly to your registered home delivery address. You can view the postal tracking consignment number directly on your dashboard.

How can I consult an agricultural expert?

Registered farmers can use the Ask an Agricultural Expert feature in the portal to submit questions regarding crop selection, fertilizer dosages, or pest symptoms. Qualified university agronomists provide personalized responses directly in your thread.
