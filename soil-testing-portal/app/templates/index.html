Why Village Soil Testing Matters

Scientific chemical testing replaces traditional guesswork with precision agriculture, protecting farmer household income and preserving long-term soil vitality.

Standardized Field Sampling

Promotes Soil Health

We measure organic carbon, Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), and pH to restore degraded farmland and maximize root nutrient absorption.

Farm Plot Identification

Informed Fertilizer Usage

Healthy soil with balanced organic carbon retains 40% more moisture, reducing irrigation frequency and saving ₹3,500 to ₹5,000 per acre in unneeded fertilizers.

Biological Soil Activity

Prevents Soil Degradation

Blind chemical dumping creates saline crusts that stunt germination. Regular testing keeps soil pH between 6.5 and 7.5 for prime biological health.
