Request Soil-Testing Appointment

Submit your farm and plot details. The local agricultural coordinator will review your schedule and coordinate on-site sample collection.

Mention plot or survey number, field name, and approximate acreage.
Include nearby landmark or road to assist the coordinator in reaching your field.
Must be tomorrow or a future date.
Time of day for coordinator visit.
Mention crop history, upcoming season, or visible soil salinity/drainage issues.
Soil Collection Field Guidelines
  • Do not sample from fields immediately after heavy rain or flooding.
  • Avoid taking samples near compost pits, manure heaps, or tree shading.
  • The coordinator will collect samples from 5 zig-zag points across your field plot at 15cm root depth.
